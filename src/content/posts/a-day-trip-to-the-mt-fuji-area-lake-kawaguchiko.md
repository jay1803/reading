## A Day Trip to the Mt. Fuji Area: Lake Kawaguchiko
### Notes Before You Go
most economical ways to travel to and from the Mt Fuji area is by highway bus from Shinjuku Station to Kawaguchiko Station.

A single journey is JPY1750 and takes approximately 1 hour 45 minutes from the Shinjuku Bus Terminal. Return or one-way tickets can be booked online on the [Highway Buses Japan site](https://highway-buses.jp/course/kawaguchiko.php).

purchasing a bus pass that gives you unlimited rides on the “Retro Bus”
### 7:45am Shinjuku Bus Terminal
the Shinjuku Bus Terminal is to take the South Exit 南口 from JR Shinjuku Station. Once you’re outside the ticket gates, walk outside to street level.

You’ll see a building opposite the South Exit that looks like this. Cross the road and take the elevator up to the 4th floor as indicated in the photo (or the escalators).
