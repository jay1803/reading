- **PageRank's Impact**: Sergey Brin and Larry Page's PageRank algorithm revolutionized web search by ranking pages based on merit, using links as a measure of value.
- **Monetization Shift**: Over time, monetization through ads became dominant, leading to a conflict of interest between user needs and commercial goals.
- **Web Deterioration**: The proliferation of ads and tracking has degraded the web, with major sites prioritizing engagement and revenue over user experience.
- **[Kagi's](https://kagi.com/) Mission**: Kagi aims to reverse this trend by focusing on user-centric web experiences, minimizing ads and noise, and aligning with the original vision of the internet.
