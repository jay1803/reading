## What Makes Notion So Good?
Community – A lot of Notion’s traffic and conversions come directly from organic search.
In fact, Notion began building its community from a few Twitter enthusiasts, according to [CRO Olivia Nottebohm](https://www.saastr.com/how-community-led-growth-drives-product-led-growth/), and brought on marketing employee no. two by finding his Notion fan blog. Since then, Notion has created voluntary positions in Notion Ambassadors.
## Breaking Down Notion’s Strategy

Following the Product Hunt launch of Notion 2.0 in 2018, Notion saw a huge amount of traffic and an uptick in users. The Notion 2.0 Product Hunt launch garnered over 6000 upvotes and is one of their all-time top posts on their site. The widespread support and the Reddit community (45K strong) rallied behind all the features Notion has to offer and its ever-increasing ability to build community and a workspace for everyone.

## COMMENTS

Notion 这类型的工具比较适合 CLG，类似的还有 Figma，Notion 社区的核心实际上是各种类型的模版，模版库的增长方式还有 Airtable，Notion 产品的功能之多让他更容易获取更多的受众。

但也如同我在 Beamer 的那篇文章中所说，宽广性很高的产品只适合中小型客户，随着客群结构由中小型客户专项大客户，产品的功能要做的足够深入才行，而要兼容不同大客户的需求，产品又要在合适的时候做的更加宽广才行。也就是产品由「小而全」到「大而专」再到「大而全」的转变之路。

Notion 实际上已经在「大而专」的阶段了，先不说它的 Table 如何对表 Airtable，或者它的 Board 如何对标 Trello，他的核心依然是笔记，而笔记，Notion 在这方面是几乎无人能敌的。
