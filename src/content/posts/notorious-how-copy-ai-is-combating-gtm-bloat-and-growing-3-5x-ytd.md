## Notorious Startup of the Week: Copy.ai
Copy.ai 首席营销官Kyle Coleman分享了 Copy 在 8 个月内增长 3.5 倍的公式：
“在过去八个月中，Copy.ai 的收入增长了 260%，其中 10 月是我们连续第四个月 ARR 增长超过 20%。同一时期，我们的 ACV 增加了两倍，交易周期时间缩短了一半。这种加速并非侥幸——而是经过计算的总体规划的结果。该计划的重点是构建比另一个软件更大的东西。我们正在创建自主的上市人工智能平台——一个不断发展的智能系统，随着更多用例的采用，它会变得更强大、更智能。

The company's transformation involved:

- Strategic Pivot:
  Evolved from a copywriting tool with 17 million users to an end-to-end GTM (Go-to-Market) AI platform
  Secured enterprise clients like ServiceNow and Siemens
- Market Positioning:
  Focused on addressing "GTM Bloat" - positioned as a critical business challenge
  Developed seven targeted "wedge" packages for initial customer engagement
- Business Model:
  Implemented a Land & Expand strategy
  Created a consumption-based pricing model
  Platform designed to autonomously learn and evolve with increased usage
