1. **Introduction**: Plausible Analytics achieved $1 million ARR as a self-funded, bootstrapped, privacy-focused, and open-source alternative to Google Analytics.

2. **Early Development**: The first code was written in December 2018, driven by a desire to create a privacy-friendly alternative to Google Analytics.

3. **Public Beta Launch**: The public beta was launched in January 2019, gaining early traction through the Indie Hackers community.

4. **Paid Subscriptions**: Paid subscriptions began in May 2019, achieving $64 MRR, with pricing designed to be accessible compared to free competitors like Google Analytics.

5. **First Traffic Spike**: In July 2019, a blog post went viral on Hacker News, causing a spike in traffic and demonstrating the potential of content marketing.

6. **Open Source Transition**: Plausible fully embraced open source in September 2019, making all code publicly available to build trust and transparency.

7. **Marketing Co-Founder**: In February 2020, co-founder Marko Saric joined to focus on marketing, significantly contributing to growth and customer acquisition.

8. **Growth via Content Marketing**: A blog post in April 2020 criticizing Google Analytics went viral, greatly increasing visibility and trial signups.

9. **Database Migration**: In June 2020, the database was migrated to ClickHouse, enhancing performance and scalability.

10. **Product Hunt Launch**: The Product Hunt launch in August 2020 provided a brief spike in visibility, though long-term growth remained steady through other channels.

11. **First Salaries**: By September 2020, the company paid its first salaries, marking a significant milestone in financial sustainability.

12. **License Change**: In October 2020, Plausible changed its open source license to AGPL to protect against exploitation by large corporations.

13. **Better Funding Model**: By December 2020, the company recognized that donations were not viable, reinforcing the need for a sustainable business model.

14. **Sustainability Achieved**: In January 2021, Plausible reached $11,303 MRR, becoming financially sustainable and recovering personal investments.

15. **Giving Back**: Starting in February 2021, Plausible committed to donating 5% of gross revenue to environmental and open source causes.

16. **Scaling Support**: With growing interest, March 2021 saw a focus on scaling customer support through better documentation and automation.

17. **Timely Content**: April 2021’s blog post on Google’s FLoC initiative showcased the benefits of timely, opinionated content marketing.

18. **Team Expansion**: In June 2021, Robert joined the team, helping handle technical customer support and development.

19. **Adblocker Study**: An August 2021 study on adblocker usage demonstrated Plausible’s superior data capture compared to Google Analytics, driving increased interest.

20. **$500k ARR**: By October 2021, Plausible reached $500,000 ARR, stabilizing its growth trajectory and refining its operations.

21. **Legal Catalysts**: In January 2022, regulatory decisions against Google Analytics boosted Plausible’s growth due to its compliance with privacy laws.

22. **Infrastructure Scaling**: February 2022 saw infrastructure scaling efforts to handle increased demand following regulatory decisions against Google Analytics.

23. **Universal Analytics Sunset**: Google’s decision to phase out Universal Analytics in March 2022 led to a significant spike in interest in Plausible.

24. **Google Analytics Import Tool**: April 2022 introduced an import tool to help users transition from Google Analytics, enhancing Plausible’s appeal.

25. **$1M ARR**: By June 2022, Plausible reached $1M ARR, attributed to a consistent marketing strategy focused on organic growth and ethical practices.
