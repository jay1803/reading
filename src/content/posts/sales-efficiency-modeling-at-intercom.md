## Key Points About Intercom's Sales Efficiency Model
Here's a concise summary of the main points:

## Background & Purpose
- Intercom began as a product-led growth company but decided to build a scaled Sales motion after their $50M Series C funding
- Kevin Kooi joined to lead FP&A and developed a Sales Efficiency model to measure performance and optimize growth

## Core Metrics
The model tracked three key benchmarkable metrics:

1. **LTV:CAC (Long-term)**
- Calculated using customer lifetime value and acquisition costs
- Healthy range considered to be 3.0x - 4.0x

2. **Magic Number (Short-term)**
- Simple calculation: Gross New ARR divided by previous quarter's Sales and Marketing spend
- Target range of 0.75x - 1.00x at maturity

3. **CAC Payback (Medium-term)**
- Measures breakeven point where Gross Profit equals acquisition and maintenance costs
- Ideal range: 12-18 months

## Evolution & Structure
- Started at aggregate level and later segmented by:
  - Business Units: Sales and Self Serve
  - Customer segments: Small Business, Mid Market, and Enterprise
- Model used uncohorted Net Revenue Retention (NRR) values to provide steady growth averages
- The team reviewed metrics quarterly and adjusted assumptions based on performance trends
