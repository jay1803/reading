The root cause is that you don't have a [growth system](https://blog.growthhackers.com/growth-is-about-systems-not-hacks-or-ideas-b4e245f457e) in place.
components of an effective growth system:
- Culture
- Strategy and KPIs
- People and roles
- Process and meetings
- Technology and Tools

If it belongs to everyone, it belongs to no one.
If it tackles every problem, it solves no problem.
If it runs occasionally, it never really runs.
