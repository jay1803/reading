## Nfx basics
Network Effects are one of the four remaining defensibilities in the digital age, including brand, embedding, and scale.

there are a few things we should point out:
1. The map we’ve laid out here isn’t meant to be taken as an incontrovertible truth — it’s a beginning point for discussion and understanding.
2. Network Effects are not viral effects. Network effects are about creating defensibility, and viral effects are about getting new users for free.
3. You’ll often see the same companies have several nfx at play simultaneously, meaning that the different nfx types are not mutually exclusive.
## Direct Network Effects
The strongest, simplest network effects are direct: increased usage of a product leads to a direct increase in the value of that product to its users.
