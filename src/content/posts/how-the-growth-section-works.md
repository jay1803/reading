### How the growth section works
#### Growth ideation and prioritization
To prioritize, the growth groups will use the ICE Framework , which consists of
the following elements, scored on a scale of 1-10:
- Impact - high score for high impact
- Confidence - high score for high confidence
- Ease - high score for ease
#### Weekly Growth meeting
- 5 min: Social questions
- 5 min: Announcements
- 30 min: Group updates
   - KPI & OKR (Live)
   - Highlights & Learnings (Live)
   - Activities (Read only)
   - Next UP (Read only)
- 10 min: Discussions
#### Other regular Growth meetings
- Weekly Growth Engineering
  - Goals: Discuss anything Growth engineering related.
- Weekly Product Intelligence and Data Weekly Sync
  - Goals: Stay in-sync for work across Product Intelligence, Data Engineering,
    and Data Analysis.
- Weekly Growth & Threat Management EM
  - Goals: Stay in-sync on growth engineering efforts.
- Weekly Growth PM sync
  - Goals: Stay in sync as a PM team, discuss PM specific issues/ideas
- Product Intelligence, Fulfillment, Data, Customer Success, and Sales Weekly Sync
  - Goals: Stay in-sync for work across Product Intelligence, Data, Customer
    Success, and Sales
- Monthly sync meeting with the Data team
  - Goals: Keep the Data team aware of upcoming changes that may impact them,
    get proactive feedback on how to set up data and tracking needs
- Monthly sync with Growth Marketing
  - Goals: Stay in-sync and work as efficiently as possible together
- Monthly sync with Product, Legal, Compliance, Data, and Privacy
  - Goals: Answer any legal, compliance, and privacy concerns
- Monthly sync with Growth section and Application Security
  - Goals: Growth / Application Security stable counterparts monthly discussion
