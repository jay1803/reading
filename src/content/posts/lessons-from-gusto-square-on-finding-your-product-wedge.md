## Summary
The podcast episode titled "Lessons from Gusto & Square on Finding Your Product Wedge" features an in-depth discussion with Mike Cherry, the Chief Product Officer at Gusto, and former head of product at Square. The conversation is hosted by Brett Person, a partner at First Round Capital.
### Key Points Discussed
1. **Introduction to the Podcast and Guests:**
- The podcast "In-depth" aims to provide tactical advice for startup leaders.
- Mike Cherry shares insights from his experiences at Square and Gusto, focusing on product development for SMBs (Small and Medium-sized Businesses).

2. **Product Development for SMBs:**
- Building products for SMBs requires understanding their unique challenges and needs.
- At Square, a significant insight was the difficulty SMBs faced with cash flow and the complexity of existing business software.

3. **Square's Strategy and Success:**
- Square's initial product was a point-of-sale (POS) system tailored for small businesses.
- The development of Square Capital stemmed from their deep understanding of SMBs' revenue streams, which banks lacked.
- Square adopted a vertical strategy with tailored solutions for specific industries (e.g., Square for Restaurants, Square for Retail).

4. **Transition from Single to Multi-Product:**
- The podcast explores the "Three Horizons" framework for balancing core product work with big bets.
- Mike Cherry discusses the challenges and strategies for transitioning from a single product to a suite of products.
- Successful transitions often leverage unique insights and data from the initial product.

5. **Gusto's Approach and Strategy:**
- Gusto focuses on helping businesses with payroll, benefits administration, and other HR tasks.
- The company has a diverse customer base across various industries, benefiting from the shared nature of many HR-related jobs.
- Gusto uses a rolling three-year vision for strategic planning, balancing core enhancements with new bets.

6. **Encouraging Innovation and Risk-Taking:**
- The culture at Gusto and Square supports experimentation and risk-taking.
- Successful innovation often comes from allowing small, bottom-up initiatives to flourish.
- Mike emphasizes the importance of creating a space for employees to experiment and not penalizing failure.

7. **Decision-Making and Resource Allocation:**
- Decisions on new product developments are guided by structured frameworks and market insights.
- Gusto and Square prioritize starting small with new initiatives to validate hypotheses before scaling up.
- The companies use customer feedback and data to inform product development and strategy.

8. **Empathy and Curiosity in Product Management:**
- Mike highlights empathy, curiosity, and good judgment as critical traits for product managers.
- Successful product managers can understand customer needs deeply and make informed decisions without perfect information.

9. **Challenges and Learnings:**
- One challenge discussed is managing the pace of execution and improving team efficiency.
- Regular retrospectives and examining decision-making processes help identify areas for improvement.
- Ensuring alignment across teams and managing dependencies are crucial for faster execution.
