Programming today is stressful
I’ve been forced to start working in sprints (usually 1-2 weeks) instead of spending larger chunks of time on larger projects.

Here's how I think sprints are doing us wrong:
### 1. Sprints never stop
Sprints are not simply shorter deadlines, encountered sporadically as you move along. They are forever repeating, back-to-back deadlines.

Experiencing stress over the long-term, however, can take a real physical and mental toll on your health.
### 2. Sprints are involuntary
If a development team were to sit down and decide to deliver code every two weeks, based on a process of their own design.

And it makes sense, doesn’t it? No one likes being told what to do—it ruins the experience.
### 3. Sprints Neglect Key Supporting Activities
Another stressful aspect of sprints in a Scrum-like environment is that they often leave you feeling unprepared for the next task.
