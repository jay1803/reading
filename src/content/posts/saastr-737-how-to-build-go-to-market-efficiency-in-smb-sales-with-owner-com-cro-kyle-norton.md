## Outbound Marketing Strategy Summary
Kyle Norton, the CEO of owner.com, has engineered an effective outbound strategy focused on scalability and efficiency within the SMB market, particularly targeting restaurants. Here are the key elements of their outbound strategy:
### 1. **Targeting and Data Utilization**:
- **High-Quality Prospects**: Owner.com emphasizes targeting high-quality prospects by utilizing data-driven approaches. They employ machine learning to score potential customers based on expected Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV), focusing efforts on prospects that show a higher likelihood of long-term value.
- **Data Enrichment**: The company has invested in data enrichment techniques to better identify and understand potential customers. This includes using web scraping and other tools to enhance the accuracy of prospect data, thus increasing the efficiency of their outbound campaigns.
### 2. **Resource Allocation**:
- **Efficient Use of Resources**: Kyle stresses the importance of efficient resource allocation, particularly in terms of BDR (Business Development Representative) utilization. By improving data accuracy, owner.com has significantly reduced the time BDRs spend on unproductive tasks, thereby doubling their efficiency.
### 3. **Focus on Deal Quality**:
- **Reducing Churn**: Early in the strategy, a significant challenge was high churn rates, especially within the first 90 days. To address this, owner.com refined its approach to focus more on deal quality rather than quantity, ensuring that growth was sustainable and that resources were not wasted on low-quality leads.
### 4. **Continuous Improvement**:
- **Adaptation and Learning**: The outbound strategy is under continual review and adaptation, with regular adjustments based on performance data and market feedback. This iterative process helps maintain the effectiveness of their sales efforts and adapt to changes in the SMB landscape.
### 5. **Scalability and Growth**:
- **Scaling the Team**: As the foundation and processes were established and refined, owner.com focused on scaling their team responsibly. This involved careful hiring based on the needs dictated by data insights and strategic goals, rather than rapid expansion.
