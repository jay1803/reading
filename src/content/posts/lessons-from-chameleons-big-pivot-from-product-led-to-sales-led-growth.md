### Deep Analysis

1. **Chameleon's Initial PLG Strategy**

Chameleon initially adopted a PLG approach, focusing on low pricing and a self-service model. However, they encountered significant challenges as this approach did not align well with their target customer base, which required more tailored solutions and direct engagement.

2. **Shift to Sales-Led Growth**
Facing potential failure, Chameleon pivoted to a sales-led approach. This strategy involved more direct interaction with customers, providing tailored solutions and support. This pivot was crucial for Chameleon’s survival and helped stabilize their financial situation by aligning their sales strategies with customer expectations and needs.

3. **User Experience and Customer Onboarding**
The transition to a sales-led model underscored the importance of user experience. Chameleon focused on enhancing customer onboarding processes to ensure users fully understood and utilized their product effectively, which in turn improved customer satisfaction and retention.

4. **Business Model and Market Adaptation**
The pivot allowed Chameleon to refine its business model. They moved away from a purely self-service model to one that combines direct sales with strong customer support. This adaptation was not only a response to their immediate needs but also an alignment with broader market trends where personalized customer engagement has become critical.
