### Inbound Or Outbound Sales – Which One Should You Focus On?
#### Cons of Outbound Sales
- Public Enemy Number One
- Interruption
- High-pressure Sales Tactics
- Time Inefficiency
- Finity
#### Pros Of Outbound Sales
- It Works
- Predictable & Scalable
- Power To Choose Your Customers
- You Determine the Timing & Medium
- Immediate Results
- Targeted Reach
- Higher Level of Engagement

There are 2 approaches to cold emails used today:
1. You're emailing someone high up in the organization asking for a referral down to the right person (aka Cold Calling 2.0)
2. You're emailing the decision maker directly pitching them to sign up/call/meeting/etc
