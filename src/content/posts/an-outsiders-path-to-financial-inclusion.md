Sebastian Siemiatkowski is the founder and CEO of Klarna, a Swedish fintech company that offers buy now, pay later services to online shoppers. He grew up as a Polish immigrant in Sweden, facing poverty, health issues, and a difficult marriage. He discovered his passion for business and innovation through reading science fiction books and working various jobs, including a stint at Burger King. He met his co-founder, Niklas Adalberth, at the fast-food chain and they embarked on a series of adventures, such as hitchhiking around the world, starting a YouTube channel, and entering an entrepreneurship competition. They launched Kreditor in 2005, a company that aimed to solve the problem of unpaid invoices for small businesses. They grew the company from a small startup to a profitable and trusted brand, using their sales skills, customer focus, and resilience. Siemiatkowski credits his parents, his education, and his co-founder for shaping his vision and values as a founder.
