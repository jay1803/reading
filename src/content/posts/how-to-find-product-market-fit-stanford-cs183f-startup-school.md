## Content
Market always won.

pre Product-Market Fit, basically save as much cash as possible, spend as little as possible and extend your runway as long as possible.
until you find Product-Market Fit

the data of your product is actually useful for other businesses to build their business on top of yours.
That's what Salesforce, Amazon, Facebook does.

> Not a platform until you've made ~$100M revenue with $1B ARR.
> -- Peter Thiel

> $0-1M...impossible
> $1-10M...improbable.
> $10-100M...inevitable.
> -- Jason Lumpkin

if you're not find PMF in first time, you got 22% to be success in 2nd time.

The market doesn't care at all what your vision of the world id, the market wants what it wants, and it will win every time.

> Product-Market Fit feels like setpping on the landmine.
> -- Dropbox CEO
