Amazon said on Monday that it would invest up to $4 billion in the artificial intelligence start-up Anthropic,

Anthropic, seen as one of the most promising of a batch of A.I. start-ups, will use Amazon’s data centers, cloud-computing platform and A.I. chips.

Amazon’s investment of up to $4 billion would give it a minority stake in Anthropic, it said.
