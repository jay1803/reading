### 🌱 Finding product-market fit (PMF)
1. 摒弃传统的产品管理。恩佐·阿维戈 （Enzo Avigo） 在 6 月的 PMF 之路上扔掉了 10 个精心制作的路线图。[他的建议](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-pre-pmf-guide-to-product-management)是：摒弃传统做法，以疯狂的速度行动，并疯狂地确定优先级。
2. Pre-test before you build. JumpCloud1 has scaled to 200,000+ organizations. Before they wrote a single line of code, JumpCloud’s founding team [wrote six different white papers](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/plg-vs-microsoft) around different problem statements they could tackle.
3. Measure product stickiness. Attio focused on the [ratio between DAU to MAU](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/can-plg-beat-salesforce) to gauge how many people actually wanted to use their next-gen CRM.
4. Create a media <> community flywheel. Keyplay founder Adam Schoenfeld attracted a high-intent audience for his product with [data-heavy decks and zero-click content](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/how-to-attract-an-audience-for-your) – all before he built a SaaS offering to sell.
### Marketing and acquisition
1. Activations >>> sign-ups. Markup tailored their paid campaigns to optimize for activations rather than signups, [increasing activations by 20%](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/product-led-marketing-to-reach-400000) with the same budget.
2. Automate outbound emails. Thena now generates the output of five to 10 sales reps without hiring BDRs – they instead leverage [outbound automation](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-outbound-automation).
3. Automate LinkedIn connections. Warmly puts their [LinkedIn outreach on autopilot](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-path-from-0-to-100-paying-customers), generating 10-15 qualified meetings per week.
4. Win Product Hunt. Reach an early-adopter audience by making a splash on Product Hunt. See how from founders at [Tango](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/from-0-to-200k-users-in-14-months), [June](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/you-have-a-churn-problem), and [Attio](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/can-plg-beat-salesforce).
5. Explore influencer marketing. At one point, 40% of Tango’s traffic came from TikTok. Today, the company purposefully cultivates its [influencer network](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/from-0-to-200k-users-in-14-months).
6. Test a referral program. The potential of referrals in B2B hasn’t been realized, but that might be changing. In the current economic climate, folks like tl;dv are [turning to user referrals](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-b2b-referrals) for a low-CAC, high-LTV boost.
7. Add an interactive demo. Pleo found that [interactive demos on their website](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/pleos-story-of-80-yoy-growth-on-the) were a big win, and these saw 10x better conversion compared to any other entry point on the website.
8. Build an ungated sidecar product. Eraser’s ungated AI sidecar, DiagramGPT, now drives [30% of new sign-ups](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/how-an-ai-sidecar-product-drove-30). And QuotaPath found success with their [ungated Compensation Hub](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-plg-for-complex-products) offering as an on-ramp to the core product.
9. Fix broken attribution models. Refine Labs CEO Chris Walker notes that there are big shifts in B2B buying, but most [marketers don’t capitalize](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-enterprise-demand) on these shifts because new channels don’t get tracked by attribution software.
### Activation, conversion, and monetization
1. Smartly iterate on your onboarding. Miro struggled to improve week 1 retention through big bang overhauls. Shifting to [smart, ongoing iteration](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-evolution-of-miros-user-onboarding) is what paid off according to their former Head of Growth Design.
2. Ask questions in your sign-up survey. There’s little evidence for a big drop-off at this phase in the user journey, says Jonathan Anderson from Candu. Get inspired by [Airtable’s onboarding wizard](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/how-to-optimize-your-free-trial), which captures user intent.
3. Consider a smart trial. Coefficient pairs smart automation with a human sales touch to drive [free-to-paid conversion at scale](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/free-to-paid-conversion-at-scale).
4. Don’t forget about email! Email is overlooked, but it’s a powerful and cheap tool. Get inspired by [Aha](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/ahas-bootstrapped-journey-to-100m)! and [Deputy](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/growing-self-serve-from-0-to-70)’s automated email nurturing.
5. Lift the credit card requirement. Uscreen got rid of the credit card requirement for their free trial. While it [cut new MRR from self-service by 40%](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/bootstrapping-to-85-million-end-users), they’re bullish on the benefits for retention and LTV.
6. Optimize your PLG pricing. Avoid common mistakes like designing pricing specifically for individual users ([see: Notion](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-plg-pricing-201)) and ignoring the admin experience around your pricing ([see: Figma](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-plg-pricing-201)).
### Sales and expansion
1. Validate where sales is incremental. Deputy A/B tested raising the self-serve threshold and have now grown self-serve from [0 to 70%+ of new customers.](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/growing-self-serve-from-0-to-70) It’s also helping their sales team move upmarket.
2. Focus sales on product-qualified accounts (PQAs). Figma scores accounts based on both [ICP fit as well as product readiness](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-product-led-sales). They adapt the sales process accordingly.
3. Tap into cloud marketplaces. Cockroach Labs [partners with cloud marketplaces](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/scaling-to-5b-with-cockroach-labs) (ex: AWS, Azure) to tap into trained sellers with pre-existing customer relationships and to accelerate sales cycles with enterprise customers.
4. Sell to Customer Success Qualified Leads. Aha! trains the support team on how to qualify free trial users and [effectively pass them off to sales](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/ahas-bootstrapped-journey-to-100m).
