doing the right thing, even when it’s uncomfortable or inconvenient.

visiting a sick friend, attending an unpopular event, or showing up in difficult situations. These acts may be minor inconveniences for us but can mean the world to someone else.

Showing up—whether at a funeral or in everyday life—represents the choice to engage with others’ lives meaningfully.
