### The Disruption Perspective: Is Traditional Software on Its Way Out?
One view of the world is that as LLMs are getting better at coding and software development is getting democratized, the cost of software trends 0, and instead everyone just spins up personalized user interfaces using LLMs for the tasks at hand.

Chris Paik in his piece “The End of Software” likened into the explosion of UGC in media, where he expects a Cambrian explosion in software that can be created at zero cost, meaning that software as a business in that software businesses getting to 100s of millions in revenue may break down.
### The Growth Perspective: AI Agents as Market Expanders
In many cases, finally, software, with the help of AI agents, can truly compete not just for software budgets but for true spend on labour.
### My Take: A New Frontier for Software
1. Ultimately, AI is still software
   As Satya Nadella noted on Microsoft’s last earnings call:
   “So to me, look, at the end of the day, GenAI is just software”
2. New opportunities are emerging and TAMs will increase
3. Internal tools will increase, but not dominate
4. Pricing models will evolve: We'll probably see a shift away from per-seat pricing towards models based on usage and work done by the software and over time perhaps even actual outcomes delivered, to better capture the value that the new age of software may deliver.
