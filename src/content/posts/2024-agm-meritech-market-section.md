### **Summary of the 2024 AGM Meritech Market Section Report**

#### **1. Public Markets Overview**
- **Market Rally Driven by AI & Tech Giants:** Equity markets are at peak levels, primarily due to a handful of market-leading tech and AI-driven companies.
- **AI's Influence on the Market:** Companies mentioning "AI" in earnings calls saw higher stock performance.
- **SaaS Struggles:** Public SaaS faced a major correction in 2022, with revenue multiples still below historical norms and slow growth recovery.
- **Growth Over Profitability:** Market values high revenue growth over free cash flow, but no SaaS companies currently exceed 30% growth.
- **IPO Performance Poor:** SaaS IPOs from the zero-interest rate (ZIRP) era performed badly, with median losses around 40%.
- **AI as a Catalyst for SaaS:** Despite challenges, top SaaS companies remain valuable, and AI could accelerate their growth.

#### **2. Private Markets & Liquidity**
- **Venture Funding Stabilizing:** Post-2021 funding levels have rebounded, with AI driving most of the growth.
- **Late-Stage Valuations Rising:** Market leaders see the highest valuation increases, with large funding rounds becoming more common.
- **IPO Market Weak:** The number of tech IPOs is at a 15-year low.
- **Unicorn Economy Issues:** Many private companies struggle to find exits, leading to “zombie” startups that survive but don’t generate strong returns.
- **Liquidity Still Low:** Despite high "dry powder" in venture funds, the exit environment remains challenging.
- **Selective IPOs Expected:** Only companies with strong financials and growth potential are likely to go public in the coming years.

#### **3. The AI Revolution & Market Opportunities**
- **AI Transforming Software:** AI is the most significant platform shift since cloud computing, expanding the software market.
- **AI’s Rapid Adoption:** AI market adoption is expected to outpace the cloud boom.
- **Who Will Win?** Incumbents (“Gatekeepers”) and startups (“Insurgents”) are both well-positioned, similar to past tech shifts.
- **AI vs. SaaS Business Models:** Traditional SaaS relies on human-centric workflows, while AI-driven companies sell automation and efficiency.
- **New Market Dynamics:** AI disrupts existing software moats, changing pricing models and business strategies.
- **Future Growth Outlook:** AI will massively increase software market size, with both established players and startups benefiting.

#### **Key Takeaways**
- The **public market rally** is largely due to AI and a few dominant tech companies.
- **SaaS is struggling** but remains valuable, with AI potentially revitalizing it.
- **Venture funding is strong**, but **IPOs are rare** and **many unicorns lack exit options**.
- **AI will be a game-changer**, transforming how software is built, sold, and valued.
- **Both incumbents and startups can thrive**, but AI-driven disruption is inevitable.
