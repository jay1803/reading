the seat-based pricing model has been the go-to standard in the SaaS industry.

### The Decline of Seats
As AI continues to evolve, it automates many tasks that once required human intervention.

examples of the pressure on seats already:
- NICE, a contact center support software, shared the case study of one of their clients which reduced the number of seats they needed from 1,000 to 750 when they rolled out NICE’s AI solution, a 25% reduction in seat volume. (h/t Ed Sim)
- sales engineers at Salesforce note that some large accounts are reducing seats by 10% because of the use of Einstein AI which is increases productivity

I think two interesting ones are worth discussing: work/usage based pricing and outcomes based pricing.
### Work-Based or Usage-Based Pricing
这在 SaaS 中不一定是一个全新的概念。许多电子邮件营销工具 （Mailchimp）、GTM 数据供应商 （Apollo） 和其他工具确实对一些使用代理进行了定价。
