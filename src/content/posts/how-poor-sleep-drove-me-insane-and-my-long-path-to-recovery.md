## Summary
The article is a personal story of how poor sleep quality affected the author’s mental and physical health, and how he recovered from it. The author suffered from insomnia, anxiety, depression, brain fog, memory loss, and hallucinations for years. He tried various treatments such as medication, therapy, meditation, supplements, and lifestyle changes, but none of them worked. He eventually discovered that he had a condition called upper airway resistance syndrome (UARS), which is a form of sleep apnea that causes frequent arousals during sleep. He underwent surgery to correct his nasal obstruction and started using a CPAP machine to improve his breathing at night. He also adopted better sleep habits such as avoiding blue light before bed and keeping a consistent sleep schedule. After these interventions, he noticed significant improvements in his mood, cognition, energy levels, and overall well-being.

The article aims to raise awareness about the importance of sleep quality and the potential consequences of untreated sleep disorders. The author also shares some resources and tips for people who struggle with similar issues.
