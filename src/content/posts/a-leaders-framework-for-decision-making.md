- **Cynefin Framework**: A leadership framework based on complexity science, helping executives categorize issues into five contexts for appropriate responses¹[1].
- **Contexts**:
  - **Simple**: Clear cause-and-effect relationships with obvious solutions.
  - **Complicated**: Multiple right answers with not-so-obvious cause-and-effect²[2].
  - **Complex**: No right answers; patterns emerge from experiments.
  - **Chaotic**: No point in seeking right answers; focus on establishing order.
  - **Disorder**: Unclear dominance of contexts; break down situation into parts.
- **Leadership Actions**: Tailoring actions to the governing context is crucial for effective decision making and problem solving.
- **Real-world Application**: The article illustrates the framework's use with a case study involving a police department's response to a crisis.

This framework aids leaders in understanding the nature of the environment and deciding on the most suitable approach to address challenges.
