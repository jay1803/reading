### This week's Insights
#### How to get more out of your SEO articles
SEO articles are not “one and done.”
It’s an iterative process that depends on how well the article is ranking and its trajectory.

Here’s Tim’s process:
1. Pop up Search Console every quarter.
2. Go through all your articles that are at least a few months old. Find articles that are trending downwards and add them to a list.
3. For each page on that list, apply the below changes depending on how they currently rank.
##### Ranking 1-7 and getting traffic
Once an article gets 100+ visitors a week change your goal from SEO increases (views) to conversion increases (sign ups, purchases).
1. Adding CTA’s/signups/relevant next blogs
2. Adding videos to go deeper
3. Creating lead magnets and content upgrades
4. Potentially adding more context to the article
##### Position 8-25
Often the article is not addressing the actual search intent. Why are people searching what they’re searching for? Are you actually answering that question or just addressing it? Are you answer their next question?
1. Core keyword in the H1/H2’s. You’re likely missing it
2. Shorter paragraphs and bullet lists to making skimming easier (improve readability)
3. More internal links
4. More focus on the key search intent
5. Expanding further to answer their “next question”
##### Pos 25+
The most common reason why it’s stuck is it doesn’t match search at all. It’s not even close. Go and take a deeper look at the pages ranking for the keyword you’re going for. See what patterns they all follow.
##### Pos 50+
Rewrite entirely. If you’ve been holding this position for your core keyword for 6-12 months. You likely need to rewrite the whole thing. No one is ever going to see this page.
