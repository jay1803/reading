The Red Queen Effect
The Red Queen effect is a concept that originates from evolutionary biology and is often applied to business and economics.
This concept is also derived from the Red Queen's race in Lewis Carroll's "Through the Looking-Glass," where the Red Queen says, "It takes all the running you can do, to keep in the same place."
