sales as a metric is a lagging indicator.
Pipeline for This Month is useful, but still dependent on how various reps get probability right.  Pipeline for Next Quarter is almost useless for most SaaS start-ups, even once you get pretty big.

Qualified Lead Velocity Rate (LVR), your growth in qualified leads, measure month-over-month, every month.

At Adobe Sign / EchoSign, we set LVR growth targets of 10%/month once we hit $1m in ARR, and then 8%/month once we hit about $3m in ARR.  The goal of 8%/month was to produce enough leads to grow the business at least 100% YoY.
