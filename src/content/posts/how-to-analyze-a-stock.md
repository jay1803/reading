### 1. What’s the business?
- Mission: the “why” behind what they do.
- Leadership: Founder-led companies or CEOs with a successful track record tend to outperform the market.
- Products: What does the company actually offer?
### 2. How do they make money?
- Revenue mix: Start by breaking down the company’s revenue streams.
