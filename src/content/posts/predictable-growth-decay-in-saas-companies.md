## Predictable Growth
CEO’s, a question that is frequently asked is: “How fast can, or should, we grow?”

three points become clear:
1. The persistence in growth rates holds across companies of varying size.
2. The persistence in growth rates holds for both fast- and slow-growing companies.
3. In a recurring-revenue business model, increasing the growth rate is exceptionally difficult; even maintaining the current level of revenue growth requires careful planning and great execution.

next year’s growth rate is likely to be 85% of what this year’s was.

There are two corner cases worth exploring: large companies growing quickly and small companies growing slowly.

small companies growing slowly...management teams are tempted to hope for (and often forecast) a sudden increase in growth rate. Our experience, though, is that low growth rates are frequently systemic to markets and are more suggestive of a constrained market size (or some other unfavorable dynamic) than a dysfunctional sales organization.
