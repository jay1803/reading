## Product-Led Growth Metrics
- TTV: TTV is the time it takes new users to reach their activation moment. The faster you get users to activation, the more likely it’ll be that they stick around.
- Activation Rate: When the user has experienced meaningful value in the product. (Sometimes referred to as the “Aha!” moment).
- PQL: A product qualified lead (PQL) is a lead that has experienced meaningful value using your product through a free trial or freemium model. These users are the best positioned for an upsell.
