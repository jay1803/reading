有限合伙人（LP）是为风险投资基金安排和投资资金的人，但并不真正关心风险投资基金的日常维护，而普通合伙人（GP）是投资专业人士，负责投资有关需要投资的企业的决定。
### How does a Private Equity firm work?
When a PE firm is established, it will have Investors who have invested their money. Each PE firm would have more than one fund.

In most cases, one particular investment won’t exceed more than 10% of the total commitments of the fund.

The investors who have invested in the fund would be known as Limited Partners (LP), and the PE firm would be known as General Partner (GP). So basically, the structure of a PE firm looks like this.
### Who are Limited Partners or LP?
The external investors in PE funds are known as limited partners (LP). It is so as their total liability is limited.

Generally, investors having an ability to put $250,000 or more are allowed to invest in PE Firm.
### Who are General Partner (GP)?
If a fund is created, then obliviously, you need a person to manage it. It is done by a General Partner (GP). All the decisions for the PE fund are made by GP. They are also in charge of managing the fund’s portfolio, which will contain all of the fund’s investments.
### How do General Partners or GP earn so much?
The answer is the returns distribution waterfall.
除了工资之外，普通合伙人还赚取附带利息或利差。因此，它是为投资收益提供资金的利润的百分比。例如，如果以 1000 亿美元收购一家公司，以 3000 亿美元出售，则利润为 2000 亿美元。附带利息将基于这 2000 亿美元。
