## Summary
The document titled "SaaStr 715" discusses the common mistakes Chief Marketing Officers (CMOs) make, based on insights from several seasoned CMOs. Here are the key takeaways:

1. **Lack of Strategic Involvement**: CMOs often rise through functional expertise and might not participate in C-suite strategic discussions. Being involved in strategy helps align marketing efforts with company objectives.
2. **Building the Right Team**: The importance of assembling a strong leadership team around the CMO is crucial. The process involves hiring individuals who align with the company's culture and maintaining high standards during rapid growth phases.
3. **Setting the Right Metrics**: Setting appropriate and balanced metrics is essential for guiding marketing efforts. Overemphasis on short-term metrics can lead teams in the wrong direction.
4. **Underestimating Brand Investment**: Early and sustained investment in brand development is often underestimated. Branding is not just about immediate pipeline generation but also about long-term reputation building.
5. **Consistent Messaging**: Maintaining consistent messaging across all channels is vital. It takes time for messages to resonate in the market, and frequent changes can dilute the impact.
6. **Technical and Operational Readiness**: Marketers need to ensure they have the right technology stack and marketing operations to track and analyze data effectively. This enables a more detailed and informed approach to marketing strategies.
7. **Leadership in Tough Times**: Leadership style during challenging times can significantly affect team morale and company performance. The advice given emphasizes the need for empathy and support rather than pressure during downturns.
