上市SaaS公司的估值有所上升（相对于增长率），这得益于人工智能的前景以及通胀的缓解。与此同时，客户不再像 2021 年那样购买软件，这使得增长变得更加困难。

大大小小的企业都推迟了购买，延长了交易时间线，并以一种我们一段时间以来从未见过的方式审查了云支出。
1. While growth is much harder to come by in 2023, there are pockets of resilience amid the doom-and-gloom.
虽然 2023 年的增长要困难得多，但在厄运和阴霾中仍有一些韧性。
2. The North Star for many has become ARR per FTE, which reflects the productivity of your team. We’ve seen big increases in ARR per FTE year-on-year.
许多人的北极星已成为每个 FTE 的 ARR，这反映了您团队的生产力。我们看到每个 FTE 的 ARR 同比大幅增加。
虽然裁员可能在过去一年中成为头条新闻，但它们确实帮助许多公司控制了螺旋式上升的烧钱率。新的北极星指标已成为每个 FTE 的 ARR，它反映了团队的生产力以及增加或裁员是否有助于增强业务。

3. Positioning yourself as “AI” doesn’t impact growth. But monetizing AI does.
将自己定位为“AI”不会影响增长。但将 AI 货币化确实如此。
我们调查的 SaaS 公司中有 77% 在 2023 年推出了 AI 功能或将 AI 纳入其产品路线图。但只有15%的人真正将人工智能货币化。
4. To drive productivity, companies need efficient product-led growth (PLG), expansion within the customer base, and improved operations.
为了提高生产力，公司需要高效的产品主导型增长 （PLG）、客户群扩张和改善运营。

One emerging metric to assess PLG performance is product-influenced revenue: how much net-new revenue is from customers who start with a meaningful product interaction before ever talking to sales.

### VALUE DRIVERS: LOW CAC + HIGH NDR = EFFICIENT GROWTH

In the early years, acquisition matters most for driving growth. 86% of new-net ARR growth comes from acquisition and only 14% from expansion.

Continued growth at scale comes from accelerating expansion revenue to complement the acquisition motion. For companies with $20-50M ARR, 38% of net-new ARR growth comes from expansion.

#### IMPROVING CAC: PRODUCT INTERACTIONS, THEN SALES OUTREACH

One emerging metric to assess PLG performance is product-influenced revenue: how much net-new revenue is from customers who start with a meaningful product interaction before ever talking to sales.
Product-influenced revenue is highest among SaaS companies with a self-serve freemium offer, representing about 90%
of total revenue. That said, offering a freemium plan is not enough. Product-influenced revenue can swing from 28%
at the bottom quartile to 100% at top quartile companies.
#### NET DOLLAR RETENTION (NDR)

Softening NDR has been a function of customers buying fewer seats (especially for the tech industry) as well as optimizing their cloud costs.
