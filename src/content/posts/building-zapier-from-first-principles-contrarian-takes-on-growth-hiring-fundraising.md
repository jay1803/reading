## Summary
1. **Early Product Development**: The founders created an initial, very rough version of the product, which needed significant user support to function.
2. **Remote Work Culture**: Zapier became a fully remote company long before it was popular, providing a competitive advantage in hiring.
3. **SEO-Driven Growth**: The company built an SEO-driven distribution engine before having a complete product, helping to attract users organically.
4. **Don't Hire Until It Hurts**: A strict hiring policy ensured the company only brought in new employees when absolutely necessary, preventing overextension.
5. **User Feedback Loop**: The company continuously improved its product based on user feedback and direct support experiences.
6. **Contrarian Strategies**: Zapier often took non-traditional approaches, such as minimal initial fundraising and focusing on product-led growth.
7. **Incremental Improvements**: Inspired by a former employer, the founders emphasized making small daily improvements to steadily advance the company.
8. **Product and Market Fit**: The company’s success was driven by a strong alignment between its product and go-to-market strategy, ensuring cohesive growth.
9. **Organic Demand Identification**: Zapier identified integration needs through community forums, responding directly to user requests and pain points.
10. **Focus on SMBs**: Despite advice to move towards enterprise solutions, Zapier maintained its focus on serving small and medium-sized businesses, which constituted its core market.
11. **Early Customer Support**: Initial support tasks were handled directly by the founders, providing deep insights into user challenges and needs.
12. **Balanced Team Growth**: The company expanded its team in a measured way, ensuring each new hire addressed a specific operational bottleneck.
13. **Resilience and Adaptability**: The founders' prior experiences with unsuccessful projects helped shape Zapier’s resilient and adaptive company culture.
14. **Continuous Learning and Improvement**: The founders actively sought out and applied management and operational best practices to continually enhance their business processes.
### How Zapier using SEO
1. **Early SEO Strategy**:
   - Before having a product, Zapier focused on building an SEO-driven distribution engine. This involved creating an app directory with landing pages that described how users could connect different applications (e.g., Salesforce and Zendesk, QuickBooks and PayPal). These pages were designed to rank well on Google for specific integration-related keywords.
2. **Content Creation Inspired by Bingo Card Creator**:
   - The strategy was inspired by Patrick McKenzie's Bingo Card Creator, which generated multiple landing pages for various types of bingo cards. Similarly, Zapier created landing pages for every new app integration, effectively increasing its online presence and traffic.
3. **Scaling the Integration Platform**:
   - Zapier launched an integration platform allowing other developers to build apps on Zapier. This not only expanded the number of integrations but also created more landing pages automatically, further enhancing their SEO strategy. This approach helped Zapier grow rapidly without heavy investment in sales and marketing.
4. **Sustainable Growth Through SEO**:
   - This SEO-driven approach meant that Zapier's customer acquisition costs were minimal, which is unusual for a B2B SaaS company. The company relied on organic traffic generated through its well-optimized landing pages, which continued to attract users and developers to the platform.
