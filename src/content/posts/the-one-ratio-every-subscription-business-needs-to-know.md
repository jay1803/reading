LTV/CAC reveals the true health of a subscription business.

For a growing business, LTV/CAC helps pinpoint weak spots and prioritize
operational improvements.

For a mature business, LTV/CAC guides the decision about whether to invest in
growth or run the business for profit.

LTV/CAC helps prioritize investment across a portfolio.
