Aaron and Altman represent two archetypes of what Silicon Valley might value.

Aaron died in 2013. He took his own life, having been hounded by the Department of Justice for years over the crime of (literally) downloading too many articles from JSTOR.

Sam Altman and OpenAI have scraped practically the entire Internet. JSTOR, YouTube, Reddit… so long as the content is publicly accessible, OpenAI’s stance appears to be that copyright law is only for little people.
For this, Altman has been crowned the new boy-king of Silicon Valley.

Altman is the template. It’s him and Peter Thiel and Elon Musk and Marc Andreessen and David droopy-dog Sacks.

“the 100 most important people working at [the company],” regardless of job title. Graham insists this is unique to Jobs, that he has never heard of another company doing it. [Dan Davies](https://backofmind.substack.com/) counters that this is
> “When I was at Credit Bloody Suisse, they used to have global offsites with 100 key employees from different levels (…) I don’t necessarily want to gainsay Paul Graham’s experience here, but if VCs are in the habit of imposing the kind of structures that he describes on their portfolio companies, then I think every business school prof in the world would agree with me that they are being dicks and should stop.”

the story of Apple becomes Jobs-vs-Sculley, rather than Jobs-vs-Wozniak
Steve Wozniak’s generational skill for building an ahead-of-its-time product. And then it cast aside Woz, because he got in the way.

YCombinator was one of the major sources of that cultural change, because YCombinator proved so effective at perpetuating its own mythology. Paul Graham developed a successful formula: bring together the best young entrepreneurs with the best potential ideas. Tell them they are special. Give them advice, connect them to funders, do everything in your power to help them succeed.

Peter Thiel is a good example — much of Peter Thiel’s vast fortune comes from having been the first guy to invest in Mark Zuckerberg.

The Cult of the Founder only really matters because of the [gravitational force of money](https://davekarpf.substack.com/p/the-gravitational-force-of-tech-money).

This is all of a piece with Andreessen’s techno-optimist manifesto and Balaji Srinivasan’s batshit bitcoin declarations.
