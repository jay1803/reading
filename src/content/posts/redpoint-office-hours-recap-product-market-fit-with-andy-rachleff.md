For consumer businesses, word of mouth is the best indicator of PMF.
For enterprise businesses, sales yield > 1 indicates PMF
Sales yield is defined as the contribution margin a sales team can generate in a year divided by the cost to field a sales team. In the enterprise world, the total cost to field a sales team consists of the salary + commission for the sales rep and the expenses to have them out in the field.
All this generally comes out to $500,000 — $600,000. To look at this another way: Until a sales team can generate gross profit in excess of $500K a year, you don’t have product-market fit.

Prove out a value hypothesis before pursuing growth.
Do less, not more.
Iterate on your audience, not your product.
Offer something delightful to a desperate audience.
Build your organization to learn vs. execute.

## Summary
The article is a recap of a Redpoint Office Hours event where Andy Rachleff, co-founder and CEO of Wealthfront, shared his insights on product-market fit. He discussed how to define product-market fit, how to measure it, how to achieve it, and how to avoid common pitfalls. Some of his key points were:

- Product-market fit is when people who know they want your product are happy with what you’re offering.
- The best way to measure product-market fit is by using Net Promoter Score (NPS), which indicates how likely customers are to recommend your product to others.
- To achieve product-market fit, you need to find a niche market that has a clear and urgent problem that you can solve better than anyone else.
- You should avoid building features that are nice-to-have but not essential, chasing multiple customer segments at once, and being too attached to your initial idea.
