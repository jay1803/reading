## Summary
1. **Identifying User Trends:** Notion focuses on identifying trends within its user base and building upon them rather than imposing a top-down approach.

2. **Encouraging User Engagement:** Notion aims to empower users to share and contribute to the community, fostering organic growth and creativity.

3. **Global Community Growth:** Under Ben Lang's leadership, the community grew to over 20 million users by leveraging the enthusiasm of early adopters and community evangelists.

4. **Community-Led Growth Strategy:** Notion included community-building as a core part of its growth strategy from the beginning, recognizing its long-term benefits despite challenges in measuring immediate ROI.

5. **Decentralized Community Approach:** The community strategy involves supporting numerous independent user groups run by ambassadors rather than a single centralized community.

6. **Programs and Initiatives:** Notion implemented various community programs including ambassador programs, influencer partnerships, and a certified consultants program to engage and empower its user base.

7. **User Conferences:** Notion organized user conferences to connect its global user base and share updates from the team and the community, which became a tradition.

8. **Influencer Collaborations:** By collaborating with YouTubers and other influencers, Notion effectively expanded its user base, particularly in new geographical areas.

9. **Consultants Program:** The consultants program, built with input from active community members, has grown significantly and supports Notion users professionally.

10. **Community Events:** Notion supports community-led events globally, facilitating regular meetups and workshops run by its ambassadors.

11. **Early Community Efforts:** Initially, Notion focused on creating template galleries and engaging super users to build momentum and excitement around the product.

12. **Use of Contractors:** Notion effectively used contractors from its community to handle various tasks like moderation, localization, and influencer partnerships, allowing for flexible scaling.

13. **Tools for Community Building:** Notion utilizes various tools such as SwagUp for inventory, Tremendous for global event funding, and Luma for event management to streamline community operations.

14. **Long-term Investment View:** Notion views community building as a long-term investment that differentiates it from competitors, emphasizing the importance of patience and sustained efforts.
