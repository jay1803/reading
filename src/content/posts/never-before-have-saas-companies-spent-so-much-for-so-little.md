GTM Spend Ratio = SALES & MARKETING divided by NET NEW ARR
what you're spending as a % of what you’re getting.

Here’s the trendline: 📈 150%, 167%, 179%, 206%, 224%, 249%, 249%, 264%

As an example, take a look at New Relic… (recently bought by PE). Over the past year, they spent $378M on sales & marketing.
$63M in Net New ARR.

In other words, $6 in spend, for every $1 of ARR.

But if the numbers don’t improve, you’ve got to do something over the coming quarters.
