### Cycle of Cloud Optimizations
Azure guided to 25-26% growth in constant currency, so they expect even less deceleration in the upcoming quarter (but remember, Q3 ‘22 was when the optimization headwinds really kicked in, so the YoY growth comparison for Q3 ‘23 is against an “easier comp”).

I think there was an expectation that Q3 would be the first quarter where things really got better.

On the flip side, ServiceNow also reported this week, and they called out a strong demand environment going into the second half of the year! Specifically, they said “we actually see higher pipeline coverage ratios going into Q3 than we saw last year.”

It’s a Salesforce case study from ‘08. The bars represent the YoY revenue growth, and the yellow line is the stock price.
The takeaway being stock prices move ahead of fundamentals, and if we’re drawing similar conclusions to today we should expect revenue growth to inflect 3 quarters after stocks inflect.
