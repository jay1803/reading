When people ride for technocapital, their motivations are usually clear. Nick Land, who coined the term, is a misanthrope. He doesn’t like humans much, so the idea that there could be an entity coming, already being born, drawing itself into existence by hyperstitionally preying on the dreams of humanity, cannibalizing their desires, wearing “the invisible hand” as an ideological skin: he’s into that. “Techno-economic interactivity crumbles social order in auto-sophisticating machine runaway,” as he would put it, and [that’s good](https://files.libcom.org/files/%5BCcru,_Nick_Land%5D_Ccru_Writings_1997-2003(BookZZ.org).pdf). You’re being colonized by an entity that doesn’t care about you, except insofar as you make a good host.

There are other technocapital advocates. [Nyx Land](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/n1x-gender-acceleration-a-blackpaper) inherits the original Land’s misanthropy and adds gender dysphoria. Technocapital is good because, in the long run, it turns boys into women, in the same way that industrial chemicals [turn the frogs gay](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1874169/#:~:text=Atrazine%20is%20a%20potent%20endocrine,the%20gonads%20of%20developing%20males.) and warmer oceans [make turtles female](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/climate-change-producing-too-many-female-sea-turtles-180967780/). Read the [blackpaper](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/n1x-gender-acceleration-a-blackpaper). “The liberation of the female sex by acceleration in general, towards maximizing productive potential under such a time that the male is no longer needed.” There’s no place in the hyperstitional future for individual action, protection of the household, physical labor, or other typically male traits: the inhuman will reproduce itself, the human will die away, and good riddance.

These boosters of techno-capital like it because it’s an end in itself, which lets them avoid asking questions about the ends of man himself. Which is why it’s odd to see the same ideas used for ostensibly pro-human political projects. Marc Andreessen cites the first Land, approvingly, in his new [Techno-Optimist Manifesto](https://a16z.com/the-techno-optimist-manifesto/).

> Combine technology and markets and you get what Nick Land has termed the technocapital machine, the engine of perpetual material creation, growth, and abundance.

Andreessen, a Straussian, likes to do this with his sources.

安德森用他来论证，只有技术社会才能将我们从那些束缚我们的事物中解放出来。这是一个巧妙的反转，但行不通。

Thinkers like Percy, or Heidegger, or Land, or Byung-Chul Han, think we’re shaped by technocapital (or by modernity, if you like).
they say: I’m not the same person as I was before I used the phone, or the food delivery service, or the chatbot.

安德森对这种动态不感兴趣，对文本的表面不感兴趣。相反，“人类的欲望和需求是无限的”，而技术只是满足这些需求的载体。这些需求会产生更多的市场需求，从而产生更多的创新，从而产生更多的欲望和需求，机器就会继续运转。

有一次我和朋友们坐在一起，听一位著名的生物技术专家谈论战胜死亡。这位技术专家谈到了他们的母亲日渐衰老，看着看着是多么的痛苦，以及如果一切顺利的话，医生很快就能在她母亲去世前取出她的头，将其冷冻，将大脑切成薄片薄片，并在桶中重建她的意识。

Andreessen dismisses my view as so much sentimental nonsense: “Love doesn’t scale.” This evening, I talked to my son, who didn’t exist in any form a year and a half ago...he’s a 150% return on familial investment, with almost limitless potential for further personal and genetic line growth.

when Andreessen lauds people and the human, it’s for their ability to be plugged into the technocapital machine. “People are the ultimate resource – with more people come more creativity, more new ideas, and more technological progress.”

他避免了一个危险的矛盾：“我们相信物质丰富最终意味着更多的人——更多的人——这反过来又会带来更多的丰富。”但这与我们在世界上每个国家看到的情况相反，即出生率随着物质丰富度的增加而下降。

需要明确的是，我在很多方面都属于安德森的阵营，我喜欢技术进步，而且我为一家致力于技术进步的机构工作。硅谷的技术乐观主义比许多意识形态项目有所进步。但我是一个技术乐观主义者，因为我认为唯一的出路就是通过，而且技术必须服务于人类的目的。如果没有更好的关于人类存在意义的哲学，也没有对人类比对技术资本机器更高的看法，它只是来自虚空之外的陆地生物的一个载体。
