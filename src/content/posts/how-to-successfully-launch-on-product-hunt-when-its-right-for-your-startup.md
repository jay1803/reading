### The hard truth about Product Hunt launches

Product Hunt is a playground for brand awareness, credibility, and social proof among early adopters and tech enthusiasts. It’s a sweet spot for startups that want to:
1. Catch the eye of tech bloggers, influencers, or investors to raise their next round
2. Drive a short-term growth boost
3. Receive genuine user feedback from tech enthusiasts
4. Leverage SEO juice from backlink from a high-authority domain like Product Hunt
### The insider’s guide to winning Product Hunt
1. Set the right goals: aim for conversion over popularity
2. Warm up your audience: start 30 days before launch
3. Be strategic about launch day: consider time zones and day-of logistics
   1. Use time zones to your advantage
   2. Launch-day logistics
4. Pick the right day of the week: your goals determine your day
5. Understand the Product Hunt algorithm: play by the rules
6. Invest in the right assets: spend time on these four elements
