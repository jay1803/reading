In phase one of the 1-10-100 rule, $1 equates to the amount it costs to verify
data in the first instance. This is the cheapest and most effective way of
ensuring you capture clean and accurate data. In phase two, the amount increases
to $10 – a significant rise compared to the $1 in phase one. This $10 signifies
the increased cost that incorrect data has on your business the longer you leave
it.  In the third and final phase, the initial $1 rises dramatically to $100,
and this figure represents the amount of money businesses will have to pay after
doing nothing at all to clean their data.
