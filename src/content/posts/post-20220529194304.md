## 数据分析的基础——统计学之描述性统计（一）
- **描述性统计 (Descriptive Statistics)**: The page introduces descriptive statistics as a way to analyze data trends using existing data, often supplemented with graphs for reference.

- **推断统计学 (Statistical Inference)**: It explains statistical inference as the method of deducing population characteristics from sample data, based on descriptive statistics of sample data.

- **数据类型 (Data Types)**: The article categorizes data into three types: categorical data (non-numeric), ordinal data (with a clear order), and numerical data (discrete and continuous variables).

- **数据度量 (Data Measurement)**: It discusses measures of central tendency and dispersion for data, including mode, quantiles, and mean, and how they apply to different data types.
## 数据分析的基础——统计学之描述性统计（二）
- **离散趋势 (Dispersion Trend)**: The page discusses the concept of dispersion trend in statistics, which refers to the degree of spread in data.

- **异众比率 (Variation Ratio)**: It introduces the variation ratio as a measure of dispersion for categorical data, defined as the ratio of the frequency of non-modal categories to the total frequency.

- **四分位距 (Interquartile Range, IQR)**: The page explains the interquartile range as a measure of dispersion for ordinal data and how it is used to identify outliers in a box plot¹[1].

- **标准差 (Standard Deviation)**: It also covers standard deviation and standard scores as measures of dispersion for numerical data, including their role in indicating the relative position of data points within a distribution.
