## Pinterest's User Forecast Model Overview

## Strategic Importance
- The model was critical for both external funding requirements and internal decision-making
- Served as a foundation for connecting user growth to monetization potential
- Enabled Pinterest to report on user growth metrics to secure funding and demonstrate business traction

## Core Model Structure

### Primary Goal
- Connect active users to revenue by translating Monthly Active Users (MAUs) into "Pins Seen"
- Track ad-eligible content by monitoring where Pins were served ("Pins Seen Part of Site"), as ads were only displayed on certain pages

### Key Metrics & Methodology
1. **MAU Tracking**
- Built on cohorted signup data
- Used MAU Retention percentage to create user engagement trendlines
- Incorporated seasonal variations and historical patterns

2. **Weekly Metrics**
- Tracked Weekly Active Users (WAUs) and Weekly Active Repinners (WARs)
- WARs served as a proxy for activation and user engagement
- WAR/WAU ratio helped indicate usage depth

3. **Pin Volume**
- Calculated using Pins Seen/MAU ratio
- Tracked seasonally and by cohort age
- Critical for revenue forecasting as pins represent core content units

### Geographic Considerations
The model incorporated country-level visibility for two key reasons:
1. Local holiday impacts on engagement patterns
2. Varying consumption patterns due to cultural differences and technology adoption rates

## Forecasting Methodology
- Used a 5% monthly growth rate assumption for signup volume compared to prior year
- Incorporated 12-month lookback to account for seasonality
- Included override capabilities for one-off performance anomalies

## Model Validation
- Compared user growth against each market's internet population
- Used WAR/MAU ratio as a primary validation check
- Monitored market penetration rates, especially in new territories

## Business Impact
The model proved valuable by:
- Creating clear connections between user metrics and monetization potential
- Enabling quick identification of unexpected trends
- Supporting strategic growth decisions
- Providing comprehensive performance visibility across business aspects
