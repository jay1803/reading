ABM is a go-to-market strategy that zooms in on individual accounts and personalizes at the account level.

 If you’re using inbound marketing to generate leads, your goal is to generate as many leads as possible and qualify them later to pass on to the sales team. It’s like casting a wide nest over a large area that will guarantee you some fish.
### So, when should you make the jump to ABM?
