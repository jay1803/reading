### How do they handle the hard stuff?
#### 1. Talk me through your biggest product flop. What happened and what did you do about it?
1. 跟我讲讲你最失败的产品。发生了什么以及你对此做了什么？
—Annie Pearl, corporate vice president at Microsoft, ex-CPO at Calendly
#### 2. What’s the hardest thing you’ve ever done?
—Geoff Charles, VP of product at Ramp
#### 3. Tell me about a time you’ve been in a challenging or highly ambiguous situation, and how you navigated that ambiguity.
我寻找那些在模糊性中寻找结构和前进之路的人。此外，我寻找寻求帮助、寻求意见的人，而不是“这就是方法”。 ”
—Jiaona Zhang (JZ), head of product at Linktree, ex-SVP of product at Webflow
#### 4. Describe a time when you were part of a controversial product decision, and what you did.
—Yuhki Yamashita, CPO of Figma
#### 5. Tell me about a time something went wrong. What happened and what did you do about it?
—Paige Costello, co-head of product management and head of AI at Asana
#### 6. What is the worst product that you’ve ever shipped?
这告诉我你有幽默感，你很谦虚，当你犯了错误时你可以指出。
—Maggie Crowley, VP of product at Toast
#### 7. Tell me about a time when you needed to disagree with your manager or fight for a position against higher leadership.
“你想要寻找有骨气但也能提出不同意见并做出承诺的人。这就是我通常寻找的东西。”
—Ethan Evans, retired VP at Amazon
### How do they think?
最强大、最有价值的候选人是善于自我反思、能够以不同的方式处理问题的人。他们打破规范，挑战假设，从[首要原则](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/first-principles-thinking)出发思考。这些问题可以帮助你找到横向和“开箱即用”的思考者。
#### 8. What’s something that everyone takes for granted that you think is hogwash?
如果没有真正的固执己见，就无法回答这个问题。因为它以“你认为……是什么？”开头。当我打破那堵墙时，我正在测试：这个人是真实的吗？因为有时我会解雇他们，因为他们没有告诉我什么新东西。但我不希望面试过程惩罚他们，这是我的“拯救”问题。
#### 9. What’s an unfair secret you’ve learned to improve a product team’s velocity and energy level?
“当我说‘不公平’或‘秘密’时，我指的不是你在 Medium 上读到的内容。我正在寻找你学到了什么，你是如何学到的，它是如何运作的，以及你如何应用它。”
—Noah Weiss, CPO at Slack
#### 10. Tell me about something you did that worked out but not for the reason that you thought it would.
—Ayo Omojola, CPO of Carbon Health
#### 11. What is an experiment you launched that had a very unexpected result? And what did you do after that?
—Hila Qu, growth advisor
### How do they build, ship, and drive impact?
#### 12. Tell me about your most significant professional accomplishment.
“了解从情况到结果的一切，以及他们之间所做的一切，他们影响了谁，他们如何影响，他们必须做出什么决定，他们遇到了哪些障碍。”
—Bill Carr, ex-Amazon VP, author of Working Backwards
#### 13. Ask them about a product they shipped or a release they led that is not cherry-picked by the person.
—Laura Schaffer, VP of growth at Amplitude
#### 14. What’s something that would not exist without your initiative?
—Upasna Gautam, Sr. PM at CNN
#### 15. Tell me about a time when you delivered something that was impactful.
—Lauryn Isford, head of growth at Notion
#### 16. Talk about a big problem that you worked on.
—Yuhki Yamashita, CPO of Figma
#### 17. Tell me about a piece of work that you’re most proud of.
—Katie Dill, head of design at Stripe
### Who are they as people?
您正在雇用一个完整的人来与公司中的所有其他人一起工作。为了了解这种动力会如何发挥作用或不会发挥作用，你需要深入了解这个  人。他们的优势是什么？他们看重什么？他们的职业生涯想去哪里？
#### 18. When I ask people you’ve worked with about you, what will I hear? What would they say are your superpowers and weaknesses?
我喜欢这个有几个原因，尤其是我经常跟进推荐人，而且我喜欢通过三角测量他们对其他人如何校准他们的认识。另外，我喜欢他们对批评的反应。有时候选人会让我感到惊讶。他们说，嘿，你会听到很多关于我的批评，我认为这不公平。这可能是一个不错的答案，但它们必须非常强大。
—Andrew Bosworth, CTO at Meta
#### 19. To what do you attribute your success?
“你不能说‘运气’。我总是想知道你的自我意识如何，以及你的好奇心如何。我认为，如果人们真的回过头来反思为什么他们会成为今天的样子，这确实能说明他们如何看待世界。”
—Eeke de Milliano, ex-Retool, Stripe, co-founder of Constellate
#### 20. Tell me about something you’re really proud of that you accomplished. Take me through the process, and talk to me about why you’re proud of it.
“我发现你可以了解到很多关于一个人的动机、他们的职业道德、他们关心什么、他们的优点，我认为如果你想了解一个人，这些都是非常重要的事情。与他们密切合作。”
—Camille Hearst, head of fan monetization at Spotify
#### 21. Fast-forward three years. What’s different about you then?
“很多人会默认告诉你他们渴望担任的角色或头衔。我真的在寻找关于个人和职业发展领域的谦逊和自我意识的信号；他们可以坦诚地表达自己认为需要工作才能成长的地方。”
—Ben Williams, product and growth advisor
#### 22. Imagine you had a really great day at work and you’re telling your partner all about it. What are you telling them?
“我试图弄清楚，如果让他们自己决定，他们会自然而然地去做什么，因为这会给他们带来回报？因为我认为充分发挥所雇用的每个人的作用非常重要。有时，如果你了解哪里存在自然奖励机制，你就可以弄清楚什么是最高、最好的用途。”
—Tom Conrad, CEO of Zero
