### 🌱 Finding product-market fit (PMF)
1. Measure weekly retention sprints to make sure your product is truly useful.
2. Run your waitlist through an email validator.
3. Don’t ditch white glove onboarding overnight.
4. Build a name in other communities.
   building a name in Facebook, Reddit, and Slack communities by actively participating and answering people’s queries.
### 📣 Building pipeline
5. Test a sign-up form over a blurred UI.
   Tom Orbach of the Marketing Ideas newsletter told me running this experiment at MineOS immediately spiked form completion during sign-up by more than 25%. (Don’t miss his other favorite marketing quick wins.)
6. Run a LinkedIn thought leader campaign.
7. See what your competitors are paying to promote on LinkedIn.
8. Don’t create a new category; disrupt an existing one.
9. Tap into signal-based warm outbound.
10. Ditch SEO-bait content in favor of original content.
### 🎯 Going account-based (ABX)

11. Shift from spray-and-pray to unified, account-based GTM metrics.
12. Hone your target account list.
13. Use the SPICED framework to orchestrate account-based plays.
14. Bring back (intimate) events.
### 💰 Turning pipeline into $$
15. Stop showing social proof on your pricing page.
16. Finally launch your reverse trial.
17. Consider charging for units of work.
18. Have the (willingness-to-pay) talk.
19. Consider an automatic fee increase.
### 🤖 Embracing automation, efficiency and PLG
20. Do something about fake accounts.
21. Revamp website CTAs, lead routing, and qualification.
22. Maybe remove your homepage entirely?
23. Revisit your automated emails.
24. Revisit your cancellation flow.
