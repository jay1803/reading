## Summary
Here is the summary of the document "How to measure product-market fit with the REV model":

1. **Vision Alignment**: Start discussions with new partners by aligning on the vision of the market's future to build credibility and strategic alignment.
2. **Introduction to Augury**: Augury is introduced as a leader in the industrial equipment field, focusing on machine health and process health using AI and IoT.
3. **Early Customer Conversations**: The importance of face-to-face, on-site customer interactions to understand pain points and validate the product's value is emphasized.
4. **REV Model Explanation**: Product-market fit is measured using the REV model—Revenue, Engagement, and Value—each with specific metrics and indicators.
5. **Product Development Journey**: The process of moving from a manual, human-based diagnostic tool to an AI-enhanced system is detailed.
6. **Importance of Early Champions**: Identifying and nurturing early champions within customer organizations can significantly influence market acceptance and credibility.
7. **Combining Sales and Customer Development**: Integrating customer development and sales efforts early on ensures alignment and accelerates learning and validation cycles.
8. **Handling Customer Skepticism**: Addressing skepticism through functional prototypes and clear demonstrations of the technology's capabilities is crucial.
9. **Metrics for Product-Market Fit**: Specific metrics for revenue, engagement, and value are outlined, with examples of how they are applied.
10. **Category Creation**: Augury's strategy for creating the machine health category and the steps taken to establish market leadership are discussed.
11. **Predictability in Go-to-Market Fit**: Achieving predictability in revenue and customer acquisition is key to declaring go-to-market fit.
12. **Design Partnerships**: Structuring customer relationships as design partnerships to co-create solutions and align on long-term visions.
13. **Resource Recommendations**: Recommended books and resources that have influenced the company's approach to product-market fit, go-to-market strategies, and category creation.
