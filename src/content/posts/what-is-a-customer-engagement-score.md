## Learn what a customer engagement score is — and how to calculate it.
  At its core, a Customer Engagement Score is a single number that measures how
  engaged your customers and free trial prospects are. Each customer has their
  own score, based on their activity and usage of your products and services.
  The higher the score, the healthier and happier the customer.
## Calculating customer engagement score
  To calculate a customer engagement score, begin with inputs — such as
  frequency of usage, depth of usage, specific actions taken, clickstream
  information, and key performance indicators. Combining all these inputs gives
  you a single number representing the sum total of your customer’s health,
  success, and engagement.
