As a quick refresher, ICE Stands for:
- Impact: How impactful do I expect this test to be?
- Confidence: How sure am I that this test will prove my hypothesis?
- Ease: How easily can I get launch this test?
### The Purpose of the ICE Score
The ICE score is not intended to be the perfect system for prioritizing
individual ideas. It is actually a system of relative prioritization.

The point is that the “good enough” characteristic of the ICE score works
well BECAUSE it is paired with the discipline of a growth process.
