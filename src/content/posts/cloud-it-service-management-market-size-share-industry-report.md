The global cloud IT service management (ITSM) market size was valued at USD 4.15 billion in 2019 and is expected to reach USD 15.65 billion by 2027, exhibiting a CAGR of 18.2% during the forecast period.

The market is primarily driven by the rising shift of startups and small and mid-sized enterprises (SMEs) towards the adoption of cloud-based applications and the increasing adoption of managed cloud IT services.

The global pandemic has led several businesses to adopt a remote-working environment. This swift relocation is managed by several IT organizations across the world.

The demand for ITSM solutions and services is increasing among industries such as healthcare and media & entertainment. End-use organizations such as Netflix, DisneyPlus, and YouTube are investing in upgrading their IT infrastructure by adopting cloud ITSM services.

Major players such as Serviceaide, Inc., BMC Software, IBM Corporation, and others are developing advanced ITSM solutions and services based on the AI platform.
